# Accept an invitation to order online

## Goal
Accept your supplier's invitation: create your Stocdup login from the invitation email,
confirm your email address, and arrive at their catalogue ready to order.

## Concept
Your supplier invites your business to order from them online by sending an invitation
to your email address. Accepting it creates your login and connects it to your business
on their account, so you see the products in your catalogue, at your prices, and the
delivery days they offer you. The invitation page shows the address it was sent to and
fills it in for you when you create your account. You can sign up with a different
address instead, but then a code is emailed to the invited address to confirm it's you.
The link lasts 7 days; after that, ask your supplier to send a new one.

## Starting data
- The Rusty Anchor Bar & Grill is a Vine & Co customer, not yet active, with a catalogue
  and a delivery profile
- Vine & Co has sent a portal invitation to orders@rustyanchor.example
- No login exists for orders@rustyanchor.example

## Steps
1. Open the invitation email from Vine & Co and accept it
2. Choose to create an account
3. Enter your details: Tom Rowe and a password, keeping the email address that's already
   filled in (orders@rustyanchor.example)
4. Confirm your email address from the email Stocdup sends you
5. Arrive at Vine & Co's catalogue

## Done when
Vine & Co's catalogue opens in the portal, with prices, and The Rusty Anchor Bar & Grill
shows as the business you're signed in for.
