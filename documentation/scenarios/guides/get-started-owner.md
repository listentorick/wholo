# Get started with Stocdup

## Purpose
- Reader: an owner who has just signed up, with no business set up yet
- Up and running means: one customer is set up to order from you on your portal, with
  your products, at their prices, on delivery days you can make, and has been invited
- Left out: delivery routes and runs, payment terms, order handling, team members, more
  products and customers. These are the Next steps, grouped with the part of the guide
  they follow on from.
- Order constraints: a product can't be made Active without a tax type, so tax comes
  before products; a catalogue needs products in it; a customer is set up with a
  catalogue, a price list and a delivery profile, so those come first; the invitation
  comes last, once there's something for the customer to see.
- Ends on the owner's side: no stage opening the customer's portal (decided in review).

## Goal
Set up your business, add a product and your prices, and get your first customer ready
to order from you online.

## Starting data
- A newly registered owner who hasn't set up their business yet (no business, no
  products, customers or settings)

## Stages

### Your business
1. Set up your business — distributor-onboarding
   Why now: everything else you add belongs to your business, so it comes first.

### Products and prices
2. Add a tax type — add-tax-type
   Why now: a product can't go on sale without a tax type, so you need one first.
   With: tax type Standard VAT, classification Standard, rate 20% (no tax types exist yet)
3. Add your first product — add-product
   With: no product type or supplier (you haven't set any up yet); tax type Standard VAT
   Skip: step 6 (you'll put it in a catalogue in the next stage)
4. Choose what customers can see — create-catalogue
   Why now: a customer only sees the products in the catalogues you give them, so
   without one your first customer would see nothing.
   With: catalogue Wine Bar Essentials containing Sancerre Blanc 2023
   Skip: steps 4–9 (you'll give it to your customer when you add them)
   Explain: with one product, your catalogue holds everything you sell. Catalogues
   come into their own when different customers should see different products: the
   step-by-step page shows two catalogues for two kinds of customer.
5. Set your trade prices — create-price-list
   With: 10% off the product's own price (there is no other price list to base it on)
   Skip: step 4
   Explain: a new price list isn't your default; a customer gets its prices once you
   give it to them, and a customer with no price list pays the product's own price.

### Delivery
6. Choose your delivery days — delivery-profile-setup
   Skip: step 4 (optional)

### Customers
7. Add your first customer — add-customer
   With: account number HW-1001; delivery profile Western Suburbs; catalogue Wine Bar
   Essentials; price list Hotel Trade (there is no default price list to keep)
8. Invite them to order online — invite-customer-to-portal

## Done when
The Rusty Anchor Bar & Grill is Active, on the Western Suburbs delivery profile, with
the Wine Bar Essentials catalogue and the Hotel Trade price list, and its portal access
shows a pending invitation to orders@rustyanchor.example.
