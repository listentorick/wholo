# Invite a customer to the portal

## Goal
Invite a customer to order from you online: send a portal invitation to their email
address.

## Concept
The portal is where your customers browse the products in their catalogues, at their
prices, and place orders themselves. The invitation is an email with a link that lasts 7
days. The customer must accept it with the same email address it was sent to, and
accepting it makes them an active customer if they weren't already. Sending a new
invitation to the same address cancels the earlier one.

## Starting data
- The Rusty Anchor Bar & Grill is a customer with no invitations

## Steps
1. Open The Rusty Anchor Bar & Grill's customer record
2. Send a portal invitation to orders@rustyanchor.example from its portal access

## Done when
Portal access confirms the invitation was sent, and its invitation history shows a
pending invitation to orders@rustyanchor.example.

## Doc notes
- Audience: distributor owner
- Mention: an invitation that has expired can be sent again for a fresh link
