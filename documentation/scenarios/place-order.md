# Place an order

## Goal
Order from your supplier's catalogue in the Stocdup portal: add products to your basket,
choose a delivery day and place the order.

## Concept
Your supplier decides which products you see and what you pay: the portal shows the
catalogues they've given you, at your prices. You can only choose delivery days your
supplier delivers to you, each with an order-by time, and if they set a minimum order
value your order has to reach it before you can place it. When you place it, your supplier
reviews and confirms it, or it's confirmed straight away if they accept orders
automatically.

## Starting data
- Peter Walsh at The Blackbird Restaurant can sign in to the portal and orders from Vine & Co
- The Blackbird Restaurant's catalogue includes Château Belmont Rouge 2021 and Barolo
  Riserva 2019
- The Blackbird Restaurant is on a weekday delivery profile, and has no orders

## Steps
1. Open Vine & Co's catalogue
2. Add 6 bottles of Château Belmont Rouge 2021 to the basket
3. Add 2 bottles of Barolo Riserva 2019
4. Go to checkout and choose the first available delivery day
5. Add the PO number BB-1107
6. Place the order

## Done when
The order's page shows it awaiting confirmation, with both products, the delivery day and
PO number BB-1107.

## Doc notes
- Audience: trade customer (restaurant, bar or hotel buyer), no technical background
- Mention: an order can be cancelled from its page until the supplier confirms it

## Open questions
