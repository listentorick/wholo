# Add a product

## Goal
Add a new product with its SKU, price and tax type, add a photo, and put it in a catalogue
so your customers can order it.

## Concept
A product is something your customers can order, with a SKU, a default price and a tax
type. A product is Draft, Active or Archived, and customers only see Active products, and
only those in one of their catalogues. A new product isn't in any catalogue until you add
it. A product needs a tax type before it can be Active. Its price is the default; a
customer's price list can set a different price for them. Photos can be added once the
product has been saved.

## Starting data
- Vine & Co has five products (Château Belmont Rouge 2021, Rioja Crianza 2020, Prosecco
  Extra Dry, Golden Ale 500ml, Craft Ginger Beer), all in the Full Range catalogue
- No product with the SKU WINE-201 or the name Sancerre Blanc 2023 exists
- The product type Wine, the supplier Southern Cellars and the tax type Standard VAT (20%)
  come with the base

## Steps
1. Start a new product called Sancerre Blanc 2023
2. Give it the SKU WINE-201, product type Wine and supplier Southern Cellars
3. Set its price to £18.50 and its tax type to Standard VAT (20%)
   Note: a product can't be Active without a tax type.
4. Make it Active and save it
5. Add a photo of the bottle
   Note: photos can only be added after the product is saved.
6. Add it to the Full Range catalogue

## Done when
Sancerre Blanc 2023 (WINE-201, £18.50) shows on the Products page as Active with a photo,
and the Full Range catalogue lists it.

## Doc notes
- Audience: distributor owner, no technical background
- Mention: save as Draft to finish a product later; customers don't see Drafts
- Mention: prices for particular price lists can be set on the product's Pricing tab
- Mention: images can be JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB each
