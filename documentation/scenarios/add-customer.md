# Add a customer

## Goal
Add a new customer: set up their business, account number, delivery profile, catalogue
and price list, and mark them as active.

## Concept
A customer is a business you sell to, such as a bar, restaurant or shop. The same
business can buy from several distributors on Stocdup, so you first search for it, and
only add it as a new business if it isn't there. Its set-up with you decides how it can
order: its delivery profile sets the delivery dates it can choose (with no profile, it
has none), its catalogues set the products it sees (with none, it sees no products), and
its price list sets what it pays (with none, it pays your default price list).

## Starting data
- A delivery profile called Standard Weekday and an empty catalogue called Full Range
  exist; Standard Pricing is the default price list
- Vine & Co has no customers, and no business called The Rusty Anchor Bar & Grill exists
  on Stocdup

## Steps
1. Start adding a new customer
2. Search for The Rusty Anchor Bar & Grill, and add it as a new business when it isn't found
   Note: the business is created as soon as you set it up; the later steps fill in its details.
3. Give it the account number VC-3001
4. Put it on the Standard Weekday delivery profile
5. Give it the Full Range catalogue, and keep the default price list
6. Finish without an invitation email, and mark the customer as active

## Done when
The Rusty Anchor Bar & Grill's record shows it as Active, with account number VC-3001.

- Mention: every setting can be changed later from the customer's record
