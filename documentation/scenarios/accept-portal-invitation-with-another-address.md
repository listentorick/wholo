# Accept an invitation with a different email address

## Goal
Accept an invitation that was sent to a shared address, such as your business's orders
inbox, with a login in your own name: create your account with your own email address,
confirm it, then enter the code sent to the invited address.

## Concept
An invitation is sent to one email address, often a shared one. You can accept it with a
login under a different address, but because the link alone could have been forwarded to
anyone, Stocdup asks whoever reads the invited mailbox to approve it: it emails a 6-digit
code there, naming the login that asked for it. The code works only for that login and
that invitation, for 10 minutes, and allows 5 wrong tries; after that, send a new code.
Once you're in, the invited address gets an email saying who joined, so an unexpected
sign-up is noticed.

## Starting data
- The Rusty Anchor Bar & Grill is a Vine & Co customer, not yet active, with a catalogue
  and a delivery profile
- Vine & Co has sent a portal invitation to orders@rustyanchor.example
- No login exists for orders@rustyanchor.example or tom@rustyanchor.example

## Steps
1. Open the invitation email to orders@rustyanchor.example and accept it
2. Choose to create an account
3. Enter your details: Tom Rowe, your own email address tom@rustyanchor.example instead of
   the one filled in, and a password
4. Confirm tom@rustyanchor.example from the email Stocdup sends there
5. Ask for a code to be sent to the invited address
6. Enter the code from the email to orders@rustyanchor.example
7. Arrive at Vine & Co's catalogue

## Done when
Vine & Co's catalogue opens in the portal, with prices, and The Rusty Anchor Bar & Grill
shows as the business you're signed in for.

## Doc notes
- Audience: trade customer (restaurant, bar or hotel buyer), no technical background
- Mention: the code email names the login that asked for it; don't share the code
- Mention: if you sign out instead, you can accept with the invited address itself (see the
  related page)
