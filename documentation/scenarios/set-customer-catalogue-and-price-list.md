# Set a customer's catalogue and price list

## Goal
Choose which products a customer sees and what they pay: give them a catalogue and a
price list.

## Concept
A catalogue is a selection of your products. When a customer orders, they only see the
products in the catalogues you've given them, so a customer with no catalogue sees none.
Their price list decides what they pay for those products; without one, they pay your
default price list. A change applies to new orders: a product already in a customer's
basket or order keeps the price it was added at.

## Starting data
- The Grand Hotel Leeds is a customer with no catalogues, on the Standard Pricing price list
- Catalogues Full Range and Featured Selection exist (empty)
- A price list called Hotel Trade exists

## Steps
1. Open The Grand Hotel Leeds's customer record
2. Go to its catalogue and pricing
3. Give it the Featured Selection catalogue
   Note: a catalogue is added as soon as you assign it; the price list change needs saving.
4. Change its price list to Hotel Trade, and save

## Done when
The Grand Hotel Leeds's catalogue and pricing shows Featured Selection and Hotel Trade.

## Doc notes
- Audience: distributor owner
- Mention: a customer can have more than one catalogue, and sees the products in all of them
