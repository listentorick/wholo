# Change how you take orders

## Goal
Set a minimum order value, choose whether orders are accepted automatically, and get an
email whenever a customer submits an order.

## Concept
Your company settings decide how orders reach you. With Manual review, each order waits
until you accept or reject it; with Auto-accept on submission, orders are accepted as soon
as a customer submits them. A minimum order stops customers submitting orders below that
value, and you can set a different minimum for a customer on their Account tab. Your
processing days are the days your warehouse handles orders, and they're used to work out
delivery cut-offs. Order notification emails go to the addresses you list each time a new
order is submitted.

## Starting data
- Vine & Co takes orders by Manual review, with no minimum order and no notification
  emails (nothing beyond the base)

## Steps
1. Open Company Settings and go to Orders
2. Keep Manual review, and set the minimum order to £50.00
3. Save the changes
4. Go to Notifications and add orders@vineandco.example
5. Save the changes

## Done when
The Orders tab shows Manual review with a £50.00 minimum order, and Notifications lists
orders@vineandco.example.

## Doc notes
- Audience: distributor owner, no technical background
- Mention: Auto-accept on submission accepts every order as soon as it's submitted, so
  you won't review them first
- Mention: a customer's own minimum order (on their Account tab) overrides this one

## Open questions
- "Company settings" covers six tabs (Business, Orders, Branding, Portal, Discovery,
  Notifications). This scenario covers the order ones; branding, the portal About page and
  marketplace visibility could be their own scenarios (onboarding already sets them once).
- Is £50.00 a sensible example? It applies to every customer's next portal order locally.
