# Invite a team member

## Goal
Invite someone to your team: enter their email address, choose their roles, and send the
invitation.

## Concept
Everyone who works on your account gets their own Stocdup sign-in, and their roles decide
what they can do. An Operations manager handles orders, customers, the catalogue, pricing
and deliveries, but can't change company settings, connect integrations or manage the
team. Warehouse staff fulfil orders and manage deliveries, and can only view the catalogue
and customers. You can give someone more than one role; only the Owner manages the team,
and the Owner role can't be given by invitation. The invitation is an email with a link
that lasts 7 days, and the person must sign up with exactly that email address.

## Starting data
- James Vine (Owner) is Vine & Co's only team member, and there are no invitations
  (nothing beyond the base)

## Steps
1. Open the team
2. Start inviting a team member
3. Enter the email sam@vineandco.example
4. Give them the Warehouse staff role
5. Send the invitation

## Done when
sam@vineandco.example appears on the team as Pending with the Warehouse staff role.
