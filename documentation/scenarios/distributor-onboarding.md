# Set up your distributorship

## Goal
Set up your business on Stocdup: your business details and store URL, then your
branding, how you take orders, your portal's About page, order notifications and
whether trade customers can find you.

## Concept
Your distributorship is your business on Stocdup: everything else, your products,
customers and orders, belongs to it, and you are its Owner. Your store URL is the
address of your customer portal, where your customers browse and order. The settings
you choose here decide how incoming orders are handled and who hears about them, and
every one of them can be changed later in Company Settings.

## Starting data
- A newly registered user who has no distributorship yet

## Steps
1. Enter your business details: business name Harbour Wines, business email
   hello@harbourwines.example, phone 0117 496 0200, address 3 Quay Street, Bristol,
   BS1 4DB, United Kingdom
2. Choose the store URL harbour-wines, and create the distributorship
   Note: lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens only.
3. Add your branding (optional)
4. Choose how you take orders: keep the defaults, reviewing each order before
   accepting it, processing Monday to Friday, with no minimum order (optional)
5. Add a tagline for your portal's About page: Independent wines for the West Country
   (optional)
6. Choose who gets order notifications: orders@harbourwines.example (optional)
7. Choose whether trade customers can find you in the marketplace (optional)

## Done when
The dashboard opens for Harbour Wines.

## Doc notes
- Audience: a new distributor owner, first time in the app
- Mention: every setting can be changed later in Company Settings, including the store URL
- Mention: every step after the business details can be skipped and set later
