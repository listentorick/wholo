# Create a price list

## Goal
Create a price list and add its pricing rules: a discount on every product, and a fixed
price for one product.

## Concept
A price list is a set of prices you give a group of customers, such as trade prices for
hotels. Each customer pays from the price list you've given them, or from your default
price list if you haven't given them one. A list's prices come from its rules: a fixed
price, or a percentage off either the product's own price or another price list. A rule
for one product beats a rule for all products, and a product with no rule on the list is
sold at its own price.

## Starting data
- Standard Pricing is the only price list, and the default
- The product Barolo Riserva 2019 exists

## Steps
1. Start a new price list called Hotel Trade
2. Create the price list
   Note: pricing rules can only be added once the price list has been created.
3. Add a 10% discount on all products, based on the Standard Pricing list
4. Add a fixed price of £26.00 for Barolo Riserva 2019
   Note: each rule is saved as soon as you add it.

## Done when
Hotel Trade's pricing rules show the 10% discount on all products and the £26.00 fixed
price for Barolo Riserva 2019, and Hotel Trade appears in the price lists.

## Doc notes
- Audience: distributor owner
- Mention: deactivating a price list removes prices for customers assigned to it
