# Create a catalogue and give it to a customer

## Goal
Create a catalogue, choose its products, and give it to a customer, either from the
Catalogues page or straight from the customer's record.

## Concept
A catalogue is a selection of your products that you can give to as many customers as
need it; each customer only sees the products in their catalogues. There are two ways to
make one. Build it on the Catalogues page when it's a range you plan to share: it has no
customers until you give it to one from their record. Or build it from a customer's
record when one customer needs it now: it is added to that customer as soon as you save
it, and you can give it to others later. Only active products are shown to customers,
even when they are in a catalogue.

## Starting data
- Products Rioja Crianza 2020, Chianti Classico 2021, Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc 2023,
  Prosecco Extra Dry, Craft Ginger Beer and Alcohol-Free Lager exist and are active
- Botanica Bar & Kitchen and Highfield Golf Club are customers with no catalogues
- No catalogues exist

## Steps
Explain: present steps 1–5 as "From the Catalogues page" and steps 6–9 as "From a
customer's record".

1. Start a new catalogue called Wine Bar Essentials on the Catalogues page
2. Add Rioja Crianza 2020, Chianti Classico 2021 and Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc 2023 to it
   Note: the products are only kept when you save the catalogue.
3. Save the catalogue
4. Open Botanica Bar & Kitchen's customer record
5. Give it the Wine Bar Essentials catalogue
6. Open Highfield Golf Club's catalogue and pricing
7. Start a new catalogue called Clubhouse Bar from there
8. Add Prosecco Extra Dry, Craft Ginger Beer and Alcohol-Free Lager to it
9. Save the catalogue

## Done when
Botanica Bar & Kitchen has Wine Bar Essentials and Highfield Golf Club has Clubhouse Bar,
each with 3 products, and the Catalogues page shows both with 1 customer each.

- Mention: a catalogue's products can be changed later from the Catalogues page
