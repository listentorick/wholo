## Product status
Where: a product's page (Product status) and the Products list
Kind: choice
- **Active**: customers can see it in their catalogues and order it. It needs a tax type first.
- **Draft**: only your team can see it; customers can't see or order it. New products, and products imported from Xero, start here.
- **Archived**: hidden from customers, like Draft.
Source: apps/api/src/catalogue/catalogue.service.ts (catalogue shows `status: ACTIVE` only); admin-products.service.ts (new products default to DRAFT, `assertPublishable`); accounting-product.service.ts (imports created as DRAFT); UI, ProductForm: "Required before this product can be set to Active."

## Customer status
Where: the Customers list and a customer's page
Kind: state
- **Pending invite**: you've added the customer but haven't made them active yet; they can't order. Customers imported from Xero start here.
- **Pending request**: the customer asked for access from the marketplace; you accept or decline it.
- **Active**: the customer can see their catalogue and place orders, and you can order on their behalf.
- **Suspended**: you've paused the customer; they can't order until you unsuspend them.
- **Inactive**: you declined their request; they can ask again.
Source: apps/admin/src/app/(app)/customers/page.tsx (labels); admin-customers.service.ts (create = PENDING_INVITE; activate, acceptRequest, declineRequest → INACTIVE, suspend, unsuspend); portal-invitations.service.ts (accepting an invitation sets ACTIVE); orders.service.ts, cart.service.ts, order-as.service.ts (ordering requires ACTIVE); accounting-contact.service.ts (imports go through create)

## Finishing a new customer
Where: the last step (Portal access) of Add customer
Kind: choice
- **Done and mark as active**: the customer becomes Active straight away, so they can order and you can order for them.
- **Done and activate later**: the customer stays Pending invite until you activate them or they accept a portal invitation.
Source: apps/admin/src/components/customers/tabs/PortalAccessTab.tsx; admin-customers.service.ts `activate` (PENDING_INVITE → ACTIVE)

## Order status
Where: the Orders list and an order's page (admin)
Kind: state
- **Pending**: the customer has submitted it and it's waiting for you to accept or reject it. (The customer sees "Awaiting confirmation".)
- **Accepted**: you've accepted it; it goes on to delivery and, if you use Xero, an invoice is created.
- **Rejected**: you turned it down; it won't be delivered.
- **Cancelled**: cancelled before delivery: by you while Pending or Accepted, or by the customer while Pending.
- **Delivered**: the driver recorded it as delivered.
- **Delivery failed**: the driver recorded that it couldn't be delivered.
- **Completed**: delivered and paid in full.
Source: apps/admin/src/components/orders/OrderStatusBadge.tsx (labels); apps/portal/src/components/OrderStatusBadge.tsx; admin-orders.service.ts (cancel from SUBMITTED or ACCEPTED); orders.service.ts (customer cancels SUBMITTED only); delivery-links.service.ts (DELIVERED / DELIVERY_FAILED); orders/order-completion.service.ts ("COMPLETED means delivered and paid")

## Order status (portal)
Where: the portal's Orders tab (status column and filters) and an order's page (status banner)
Kind: state
- **Awaiting confirmation**: you've placed it and your supplier hasn't confirmed it yet; you can still cancel it from its page.
- **Accepted** (on the order's page: **Confirmed**, with the date): your supplier has confirmed it, and it will be delivered on the day you chose. You can no longer cancel it yourself.
- **Rejected**: your supplier turned it down; it won't be delivered.
- **Cancelled**: it was cancelled, by you while it was awaiting confirmation or by your supplier; it won't be delivered.
- **Delivered**: the driver recorded it as delivered.
- **Delivery failed**: the driver recorded that it couldn't be delivered.
- **Completed**: delivered and paid in full.
Source: apps/portal/src/components/OrderStatusBadge.tsx (list labels); apps/portal/src/app/[distributorSlug]/orders/page.tsx (filters: All, Awaiting confirmation, Accepted, Delivered, Completed, Cancelled); apps/portal/src/app/[distributorSlug]/orders/[orderId]/page.tsx (banner "Awaiting Confirmation", "Confirmed on …"; Cancel Order only while SUBMITTED); orders.service.ts (customer cancels SUBMITTED only)

## Invoice (portal orders list)
Where: the INVOICE column of the portal's Orders tab, and the Invoice card on an order's page
Kind: state
- **Not yet raised**: your supplier hasn't created an invoice for this order yet.
- **Raising invoice…**: your supplier's accounting system is creating the invoice.
- **Raised** (sometimes with the accounting system's own status): the invoice exists; payment details appear once your supplier's system has reported them.
- **Export failed**: creating the invoice in your supplier's accounting system didn't work, so there's no invoice yet.
- **Due <date>** or **Unpaid**: the invoice is waiting for payment.
- **Part paid · <amount> due**: some of it is paid; the amount shown is still owed.
- **Overdue · <amount> due**: the due date has passed and the amount shown is still owed.
- **Paid**: paid in full.
- **Invoice voided**: your supplier cancelled the invoice.
Source: apps/portal/src/lib/invoice-label.ts (labels; ADR-072); invoices are created by the supplier's accounting integration when an order is accepted (see Invoice creation)

## Price list rule: price
Where: a price list's Pricing rules (Add rule)
Kind: choice
- **Fixed**: the customer pays exactly this unit price.
- **% Discount**: the customer pays the base price less this percentage. The base is either the **Product price** (the price on the product) or **Another list** (what that price list would charge).
Source: apps/admin/src/components/price-lists/PriceListForm.tsx; price-resolution.service.ts (FIXED_PRICE, PERCENTAGE_DISCOUNT with PRODUCT_PRICE or PRICE_LIST base)

## When payment is due
Where: a payment term's page
Kind: choice
- **On the invoice date (due immediately)**: due the day the invoice is raised.
- **_n_ days after the invoice date**: due that many days after the invoice.
- **_n_ days after the end of the invoice month**: due that many days after the last day of the month the invoice is in.
- **The next _day_ after the invoice date**: due on the next of that weekday.
- **The next _n_ of the month after the invoice date**: due on that day of the following month; 31 means the last day, in shorter months too.
Source: UI, apps/admin/src/components/payment-terms/PaymentTermForm.tsx

## Xero item status
Where: Integrations → View synced data → Products
Kind: state
- **Already linked**: the Xero item is linked to a Stocdup product.
- **Suggested match**: Stocdup found a product that looks like the same thing (its SKU matches the item code); confirm it or choose another.
- **Conflict**: the suggested product is also suggested for another Xero item; pick the right one by hand.
- **Ready to import**: no matching product; you can create one from it.
- **Ignored**: you chose to leave it out.
- **Not sold**: a purchase-only item in Xero, so it isn't offered for import.
- **No longer in provider**: it has gone from Xero.
Source: apps/api/src/accounting/accounting-product.service.ts (`formatProduct`); accounting/matching/accounting-product-matcher.service.ts (match reasons); apps/admin/src/components/integrations/products/AccountingProductsTable.tsx (labels)

## Team role
Where: Team (Invite team member, a team member's roles)
Kind: choice
- **Owner**: full access, including the team and company settings. Can't be given by invitation.
- **Operations manager**: orders, customers, catalogue, pricing, tax types and deliveries; can import accounting data. Can't change company settings, connect integrations or manage the team.

## Portal invitation
Where: a customer's Portal Access tab (Invitation history)
Kind: state
- **Pending** (shown with "Expires …"): sent and not yet used; the link works for 7 days.
- **Accepted** (shown with the date, and "Accepted by" the person's name and email): the customer used the link, and is now an Active customer. The person who accepted can have a different address from the one invited, if they confirmed it with a code sent to the invited address.
- **Expired**: the link can no longer be used, because 7 days passed or a newer invitation to the same address replaced it. Send a new one for a fresh link.
Source: apps/admin/src/components/customers/tabs/PortalAccessTab.tsx (Expired and Revoked both shown as "Expired"); admin-customers.service.ts (7-day expiry; a new invitation revokes the earlier pending one); portal-invitations.service.ts (only PENDING, unexpired invitations can be accepted; accepting sets the customer ACTIVE and records who accepted, ADR-081); customers.service.ts (acceptedAt, acceptedBy)
