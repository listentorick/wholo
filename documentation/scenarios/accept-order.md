# Review and accept a new order

## Goal
Find an order that's waiting for your review, check its products, delivery date and PO
reference, and accept it.

## Concept
An order a customer places waits for your review, unless you've chosen to accept orders
automatically (for everyone, or for one customer). Reviewing is your chance to check
you can supply it before you commit to it. Accepting is the commitment: the order is
added to a delivery run on the customer's route, its payment terms are fixed, and if
you've connected an accounting system, an invoice is created there. If you can't supply
it, reject it instead.

## Starting data
- The Blackbird Restaurant (account VC-1001) is a customer of Vine & Co, on the
  Standard Weekday delivery profile
- Blackbird has one order awaiting review, ORD-2026-50001, placed by Peter Walsh, for
  delivery next Thursday, with PO reference BB-2207: 12 × Château Belmont Rouge 2021,
  6 × Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc 2023 and 6 × Prosecco Extra Dry
- Vine & Co accepts orders manually (the default; Blackbird has no override)

## Steps
1. Find the orders that are waiting for your review
2. Open The Blackbird Restaurant's order
3. Check the products, quantities, delivery date and PO reference
4. Accept the order
   Note: Rejecting or cancelling instead asks for confirmation and can't be undone.

## Done when
The order shows as Accepted, and its timeline records who accepted it.
