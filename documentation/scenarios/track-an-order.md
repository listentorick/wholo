# Track an order

## Goal
See where your orders with a supplier stand: whether each one has been confirmed, and
when it will be delivered.

## Concept
Each order you place goes to your supplier to confirm. Until they do, it's awaiting
confirmation and you can still cancel it yourself; once they've confirmed it, it's on its
way to being delivered on the day you chose. Your orders list shows every order's status
and delivery date, so you can check before chasing your supplier.

## Starting data
- Peter Walsh at The Blackbird Restaurant can sign in to the portal and orders from Vine & Co
- The Blackbird Restaurant has two Vine & Co orders: one accepted, for delivery next
  Tuesday, with PO reference BB-2214; one awaiting confirmation, for delivery next
  Thursday, with PO reference BB-2215

## Steps
1. Open your orders with Vine & Co
2. Show only the orders Vine & Co has accepted
3. Open the order with PO reference BB-2214

## Done when
The order shows it's confirmed, with the date it was confirmed, its delivery date and
address, and its products.
